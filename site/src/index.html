What a build covers

One SCP roleplay server, set up properly. We work to what's written in your ticket, and anything outside that comes back to you before it gets built.

  • ChannelsLaid out by department, with the permissions actually set, not left on default.
  • RolesClearance levels, MTF units, staff ranks, and the hierarchy that makes them work.
  • CategoriesContainment, research, security, off-site, arranged so members can find things.
  • Lore fitNaming and structure that matches your site's story, not a generic template.
  • HandoverAdmin access removed the moment we're done. We tell you when to revoke it.
  • AfterwardsCome back any time. Your old builds are on file and we can pick up from them.

How it works

Three things happen on your side. The rest is ours.

  1. Open a ticket

    Answer four questions about the server you want. A builder claims it, usually the same day.

  2. Sign and give access

    You read a short agreement and press accept, then hand your builder an admin role so they can work live.

  3. Take it back

    Build finished, access revoked, ticket closed. Leave a rating if you feel like it.

See every step of the process, including what happens if something goes wrong.

Join the team

We take builders and moderators. You apply in Discord, the bot asks you a set of questions, and Head Staff read your answers. Accepted applicants come in as Trainee and run one drill: a full practice case with a staff member playing the client. Pass it and you're a full Builder or Moderator.

No experience running a public server is required for moderation. For building, show us something you've made.

Ready when you are

Open a ticket in our Discord and tell us about your site. If nobody claims it within two days, the bot apologizes and closes it, so you're never left waiting on silence.
